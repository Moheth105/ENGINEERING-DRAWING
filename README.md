## Abstract

This project presents the design and fabrication of a simple wooden book stand made from wooden boards and support pieces.

The book stand consists of a stable base board, an inclined support board, a rear support and a front retaining strip. The inclined support provides a suitable position for keeping a book while reading or studying.

The base board acts as the structural foundation of the stand. The wooden components are joined using wood adhesive and reinforcing screws where required.

The project demonstrates basic carpentry operations such as marking, measuring, cutting, shaping, joining, assembly, sanding and finishing.

The completed wooden book stand provides a simple, useful and stable support for holding books in an inclined position.

## Working Principle

The base board provides the foundation for the complete wooden book stand.

An inclined support board is positioned above the base at a suitable angle. This inclined surface provides a convenient position for placing a book during reading or studying.

A front retaining strip is fixed near the lower edge of the inclined support. It prevents the book from sliding forward while it is placed on the stand.

A rear support or wooden block is provided behind the inclined board. It maintains the required angle and provides additional stability to the structure.

The wooden components are joined using wood adhesive and reinforced with wood screws where required.

When a book is placed on the inclined support, the weight of the book is transferred through the support structure to the base. The front retaining strip keeps the book securely in position.

## Fabrication Procedure
### Step 1 – Material Selection

A suitable, well-seasoned wooden board free from major knots, cracks and defects is selected for fabricating the book stand.

### Step 2 – Marking and Measurement

The required dimensions of the base board, inclined support, front retaining strip and rear support are marked using a measuring scale, try square and pencil.

### Step 3 – Cutting

The marked wooden pieces are cut to the required dimensions using a suitable hand saw or tenon saw.

The pieces are checked after cutting to ensure that the required dimensions are maintained.

### Step 4 – Shaping

The cut edges and corners are shaped and smoothed using suitable carpentry tools such as a chisel and mallet.

Sharp and rough edges are removed to improve safety and appearance.

### Step 5 – Support Preparation

The rear support is prepared to provide the required inclination to the main support board.

The position and angle of the support are checked before assembly.

### Step 6 – Joint Preparation

The mating surfaces of the base, inclined support and rear support are prepared and checked for proper contact and alignment.

### Step 7 – Assembly

The inclined support and rear support are fixed to the base board using wood adhesive.

Wood screws are used to reinforce the joints where required.

### Step 8 – Retaining Strip Fixing

The front retaining strip is fixed near the lower edge of the inclined support.

It acts as a stopper and prevents the book from sliding down.

### Step 9 – Finishing

The complete assembly is sanded to remove rough edges, splinters and uneven surfaces.

A wood polish or varnish is then applied to protect the wooden surface and improve its appearance.

### Step 10 – Final Inspection

The completed book stand is checked for:

Stability
Support alignment
Inclination
Strength of joints
Retaining strip position
Surface finish
Ability to hold a book securely

## Maintenance
Wipe the surface periodically using a dry or slightly damp cloth.
Avoid prolonged exposure to moisture.
Keep the wooden stand in a dry place.
Check the joints periodically for looseness.
Tighten screws if required.
Re-glue loose joints when necessary.
Re-apply wood polish or varnish periodically.
Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and heat.
## Safety Precautions
Use saws and chisels carefully.
Always cut away from the body.
Wear safety goggles while cutting, sanding or chiseling.
Clamp the workpiece securely before cutting or shaping.
Keep fingers away from blades and sharp edges.
Handle drilling tools carefully.
Avoid excessive force while using hand tools.
Keep the working area clean.
Store tools safely after use.
## Conclusion

The project presents the design and fabrication of a simple and practical wooden book stand.

The book stand consists of a base board, inclined support board, front retaining strip and rear support. These components work together to provide a stable and convenient position for holding books.

The base board provides the structural foundation, while the inclined support holds the book at a suitable reading angle. The front retaining strip prevents the book from sliding, and the rear support provides additional stability.

The project demonstrates basic carpentry operations including marking, measuring, cutting, shaping, jointing, assembly, sanding and finishing.

It also provides practical exposure to the tools, materials and techniques used in Engineering Practices Laboratory carpentry work.
